# Recruitment and progression continuation

This update continues the uploaded `Avelune_ The Road Beyond the World.html`. Its artwork matches the repository baseline; its additional rivers, collision edits and connected bridge rendering are preserved. The uploaded SHA-256 is `78c42bb2566038e45ed795d3c093484bfbe57cc1e201ca55314460b1444ec5e6`. Tile differences are stored in `world.json` as `uploadedWorldOverrides` and applied after overworld generation.

Roadkeeper starts alone. Completing the bridge brace recruits Mara, speaking with Iven at the clinic recruits Iven, and reading Sella's chart recruits Sella. Job plans are fixed before starting but do not make future companions active. Recruitment is idempotent and saved. Old automatic-party journals retain jobs, stats and equipment in the roster; companions without recruitment flags wait to join. A companion's reserved equipment cannot be sold or duplicated while they are waiting.

Town service people are indoors. All 18 settlements have real signed shop doors; the town directory walks through a door and then to its physical keeper. Exterior signs render no people. Existing story interiors are retained and 103 additional shop interiors are provided. Wyla remains her original story character, and Mara, Iven and Sella disappear from their story positions after recruitment. Camps use nonhuman campsite markers.

Eight ranked families replace overlapping spell entries: Ember, Frost, Bolt, Gale, Stone, Tide, Starlight and Mend. A family has one current menu entry, such as Bolt I → Bolt II → Bolt III. Rank II requires level 5 and 180 G; rank III requires level 12 and 480 G. Elemental rank II/III attacks strike all foes with greater power and MP cost. Mend's ranks improve single-ally healing. Ward, Renewal, Rekindle and Comet keep distinct utility roles. Legacy spell aliases collapse into the appropriate family and preserve earned ranks.

Attacks develop through character levels and equipment, following FF1's broad physical-growth model rather than FF2's repeated-use leveling. Attack ranks I–IV unlock at levels 1, 5, 12 and 20. The battle log reports their combined multi-hit damage. These are original streamlined values, not an exact NES accuracy or hit formula.

Fourteen consumables now support different roles. Alongside the original six are High ether, Hearth elixir, Morning dew, Smoke bomb, Ward tonic, Ember bomb, Frost shard and Storm orb. They provide stronger mana recovery, mixed full recovery, party healing, ordinary-encounter escape, three-round protection and elemental damage. Invalid/full recovery keeps the item; smoke cannot bypass a guardian; group items consume one copy. HP and MP recovery replay as separate resources in battle.

The build contains 211 authored/local scenes plus 32 overworld shores. All original 91 story events remain. Node regression coverage includes recruitment, saved future jobs, old journal migration, all six solo jobs defeating the introductory guardian, spell ranks, attack growth, all new item effects, indoor counters and preserved uploaded tiles. Browser checks exercise the actual recruitment interactions, shop-door navigation, spell purchases, elemental item targeting, saves and 320/390px layouts.
